GENLAYER DELEGATED BUDGET LEDGER

FeePilot v3

Give named AI executors a bounded GEN allowance without surrendering the full reserve. Every spend is independently assessed by GenLayer validators, finalized, and written into a durable on-chain ledger.

The interface uses GenLayerJS for writes, estimates the network fee, waits for consensus finality, then re-reads the exact budget or spend record from final state. A wallet receipt alone never counts as settlement.
